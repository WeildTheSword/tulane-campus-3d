What you're looking at

A voxel reconstruction of the uptown campus: St. Charles Avenue and Audubon Park at the front, Freret and Willow streets crossing the campus, the athletics complex back at Claiborne. Building positions come from campus maps plus published coordinates for Yulman Stadium, Turchin Stadium and Devlin Fieldhouse; footprints and heights are estimates, so treat this as a blocking-out pass, not a survey.

To make it survey-accurate, load real building footprints from OpenStreetMap below. The importer extrudes every footprint to scale, draws the streets, streetcar tracks and trees, and keeps the same voxel look.
